Features

Everything in the app

Based on what FocusFlow actually ships today—no ads, no filler features.

⏱

Pomodoro that fits your day

Focus, short break, and long break cycles with per-task linking. Tune durations globally or per task, pause, skip, and track cycles—powered by a native timer on iOS.

✓

Tasks & schedule

Daily task lists with priorities, categories, weekly repeats, reminders, and Pomodoro goals per task.

▤

Today’s Flow

A timeline for your day—timed tasks and flexible blocks packed so you always know what’s next.

🔥

Streaks & daily goals

Hit task or focus-hour goals, build streaks with milestones, and open a dedicated streak view with week-at-a-glance badges.

◎

Study groups

Create or join with a 6-character code. See who’s focusing on the shared desk—working, on break, or idle—with live 🍅 counts, mood avatars, and today / week / year rankings. Presence only; no chat.

↗

History & analysis

Day, week, month, and year charts plus breakdowns by task and category—see focus time, completions, and skips.

◉

Live Activity & notifications

See your timer on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, with local notifications when a focus or break phase ends.

◇

Sync & privacy

Sign in to sync tasks and history across devices via Firebase, or try guest mode locally. No ads, no third-party analytics. Privacy Policy.
