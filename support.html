Common topics

  • Sync issues

    If tasks or focus history do not appear on another device, confirm you are signed in with the same account on both devices and have an internet connection. Sign out and back in only if needed. If problems continue, email us with both devices’ iOS versions.

  • Live Activity issues

    Live Activity requires a supported iPhone and iOS version. Check that FocusFlow has permission for Live Activities in Settings. If the timer looks stuck, force-quit and reopen the app, then start a new session. Live Activity reflects the app’s current timer state—it is not a separate service.

  • Deleting app data

    Removing the app from your device deletes local data stored only on that device. Cloud data tied to a signed-in account may remain until you request deletion. See our Privacy Policy for details.

  • Account and sign-in

    Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication (email and password). Use “Forgot password” in the app if available, or contact us if you cannot access your account. We cannot see your password.

  • Feedback and bug reports

    We welcome suggestions and bug reports. Tell us what you expected, what happened, and steps to reproduce. We read every message but may not reply to every suggestion.
