Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

FocusFlow (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a productivity app for focus sessions, tasks, and progress tracking. This policy describes what information the app may store and how it is used. We aim to be clear and honest—we do not claim perfect anonymity or military-grade encryption.

Summary: FocusFlow has no advertising, no third-party analytics, and we do not sell your personal data.

What data the app stores

Depending on how you use FocusFlow, the app may store:

  • Tasks and categories — titles, organization, and related settings you create.
  • Focus and pomodoro history — session lengths, counts, and statistics derived from your usage.
  • App settings — preferences such as timer durations, sounds, and display options.
  • Progress and stats — aggregated views of your focus activity shown in the app.

Local storage

Much of this data is kept on your device using local storage so the app can work offline and respond quickly. Data stored only on your device remains under your control and is removed when you delete the app (unless you also use cloud sync, described below).

Account sign-in and cloud sync

If you choose to create an account and sign in, FocusFlow uses Google Firebase to authenticate you and sync selected app data across your devices. In that case, the same types of data listed above (tasks, categories, focus history, settings, and stats) may be stored on Firebase servers associated with your account.

We use Firebase only for authentication and data sync—not for ads or cross-app tracking. Firebase operates under Google’s terms and privacy practices. You can read more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

If you use the app without signing in, cloud sync does not apply and your data stays on the device for that usage mode.

Live Activity and notifications

On supported Apple devices, FocusFlow may show your current timer state through Live Activity (Lock Screen / Dynamic Island) and local notifications. These features use timer and session state on your device to display accurate information. They are not used for marketing or advertising.

What we do not do

  • We do not show third-party advertisements in the app.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your data for unrelated marketing purposes.

Data security

We rely on platform and Firebase security measures that are standard for mobile apps. Passwords are handled through Firebase Authentication and are not stored by us in plain text. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we work to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

FocusFlow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can stop using cloud sync by signing out or not creating an account. You may delete local app data by removing the app from your device. To request account or cloud data deletion, contact us at the email below and we will help where applicable.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
